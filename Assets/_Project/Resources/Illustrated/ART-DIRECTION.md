Exactly 4 columns by 4 rows, equally spaced 256x256 cells in a 1024x1024 image. Every sprite centered in its own cell with 24 pixels empty margin. Crisp PIXEL ART, explicit square pixels, 64x64 native pixel detail enlarged 4x nearest-neighbor, no smooth illustration. Closely match My Heroes Dungeon Raid and Brave Frontier sprite feel: rich jewel colours, deep ink outlines, intricate metallic edges, small strong highlights, dense readable silhouettes. Row1 left-to-right: ornate cyan ice curved sword; red-gold flaming greatsword; emerald thorn bow; violet crescent scythe. Row2: sapphire wizard staff with floating crystal; bronze celestial hammer; crimson horned helmet; ornate blue-gold breastplate. Row3: emerald gemstone necklace; violet arcane ring; glowing orange fire spell icon; cyan three ice-spears spell icon. Row4: emerald healing leaf spell icon; violet rune spell icon; golden lightning spell icon; red-black shield spell icon. Weapons should occupy most of cell diagonally. Gear detailed and beautiful with multiple colour ramps not plain geometric shapes. Spell icons are circular compact compositions with dark circular backing and bright magical forms, no surrounding UI frames. All 16 distinct sprites must fit exactly within their cells. These are runtime sprites, not a concept presentation.

Exactly 3x3 regular equal cells on square canvas, 9 complete individual tiny heroes, each same size and position within cell.
